Bill Drafter for Word

A demo Word add-in: amendments from your own copy of the laws, standard sections, drafting checks, clean copies, Akoma Ntoso export, and changes to budget bills with LBDC's budget checks.

Download manifest.xml

Add it to Word for Windows

  1. Save manifest.xml in a folder on a network share, e.g. \\server\share\Word add-ins.
  2. Word: File > Options > Trust Center > Trust Center Settings > Trusted Add-in Catalogs. Add the folder's network path, tick Show in Menu, restart Word.
  3. Home > Add-ins > More Add-ins > Shared Folder > Bill Drafter (demo) > Add.

Word on the web: Home > Add-ins > More Add-ins > My Add-ins > Upload My Add-in, then pick manifest.xml.

Drafting a bill

  1. Open Home > Bill Drafter. Click Load the Bank at the top right and choose the folder with the law files. They stay on this computer.
  2. On Draft, type what the bill is in relation to and click Start the bill. Add sections: type the law and section in one box (GCN 12, tax 606(c)), choose what the bill does, insert it.
  3. Edit the law's text in the document, then click Mark changes: brackets and underlines are put in against the Bank. The section list shows what each section still needs.
  4. On Clean copy, click Make the clean copy (PDF). Changes are always marked against the Bank first.

Changing a budget bill

  1. Click Budget at the top right and open a demo bill (the 2025 Aid to Localities bill, whole or in part, or State Operations).
  2. Edit items in the document and click Mark changes: reappropriations are bracketed and underlined against the law, appropriations take the new amount. Totals that no longer add up can be updated.
  3. Check runs LBDC's budget checks on the whole bill: amounts, last year's chapter, changes in this print, line vetoes. Open the bill window browses and searches the whole bill.
