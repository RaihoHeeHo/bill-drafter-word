Bill Drafter DEMO

Getting started

  1. Load the Bank, the laws this computer drafts from.
  2. Start a bill below.

Then add sections, edit the law's text in the document as you would any text, and press Mark changes: brackets and underlines are put in for you.

    Add a section

    The Bank is empty on this computer, so there are no laws to amend yet.
      Show the law's text

      Type the law and the section, then pick it from the list. You can type the law's ID or a word of its name, and a subdivision: GCN 12, executive 296(1), tax 606(c).

      What the bill does

      The Bank does not hold a section's history. You can add it later from the section list.

      Short title · always section 1
      Legislative findings · after the short title
      Severability clause · before the effective date
      Blank section · new law in your own words
      Effective date · always last
      Add-ons

      Each section goes where the Drafting Manual puts it, and the sections are renumbered.

      Tables

      A table in the law's text is a real Word table. Change it in the document, or here: edit it as the law will read, and the brackets and underlines are put in when you save.

        Table

        Each row's kind: Row, Headings (the column headings), Group ("Group A:", across the table) or Note ("{3} …", under the table). Enter in a cell starts a new line in it.

        Open a budget bill

        The bill is laid out in this document from its Akoma Ntoso XML, which is kept in the document. You make changes in the document; the checks, the totals and the clean copy all work from the XML.

        This document already has text, so the budget bill will go after it. Open a blank document first for a clean start.

        Making changes how edits are read

        Edit an item in the document as you would any text, then press Mark changes.

        • Reappropriations continue existing law: changed words and original amounts are put in [brackets] and underlined. The amount reappropriated, "(re. $…)", is this year's figure and is just reprinted.
        • Appropriations are new money in this bill: they take the new words and amount as typed.
        • Totals and agency summaries that no longer add up are listed above, with a button to update them.

          New money in this bill: printed as typed, not underlined. Put the program code in parentheses at the end of the words.

          Amounts

          The checks of the print-error report: program totals, subtotals and agency summaries against the items, project schedules against their rows and their item, and reappropriations against their original amounts.

            Last year's chapter

            Every reappropriation is looked up in the chapter it continues, by agency, account, program and object: its original amount must be what that chapter appropriated, and the amount reappropriated no more than it.

            Use another chapter

            Changes in this print

            Items added, removed, recoded or reworded, amounts changed, and totals changed, matched by their key.

            Line vetoes

            The Division of the Budget's list cites each veto by the bill's page and lines. Each is placed on the item printed wholly on those lines, its words and amounts compared, and its line veto memorandum written.

            Check the bill

            The title and enacting clause, section numbers and order, each amendment against the Bank (citation, history, marking), and style in new matter.

              Clean copy

              People

              Word's Track Changes are suggestions. Everyone who made one, or a comment, takes part in the review as a drafter, examiner or proofreader. The roles are saved in the document.
