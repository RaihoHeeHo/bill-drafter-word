    What the bill does

    The Bank does not hold a section's history, so type its last amendment here. It goes into the next section you insert, or click in a section already in the bill and press:

    Then edit the law's text right in the document, as if it were plain text. Mark changes compares it with the Bank and brackets what you took out and underlines what you added. Word's own Track Changes may be on or off.

    Standard sections

    Sections go where the Manual puts them: the short title first, findings next, severability just before the effective date, the effective date last. Numbers follow.

    Sections

      Drafting checks

      Structure (title, enacting clause, numbers, order of the standard sections), amendments (citations, history, marking checked against the Bank) and style in new matter.

        Bill information

        Goes into the Akoma Ntoso front matter and LBDC metadata (AKN-NY profile 0.3), so the clean-copy template can print the cover.

        Akoma Ntoso

        Converts this document to Akoma Ntoso 3.0 XML in the shape of LBDC's AKN-NY profile (version 0.3): front matter, each amendment as a <mod> whose lead-in cites the law by its Bank ID, the amended text in a <quotedStructure> with New York's unit names, new matter as <ins> and old matter as <del>.

        Clean copy

        Typesets the Akoma Ntoso with the LBDC clean-copy template, which matches the public PDFs line for line: bold front matter, numbered lines, new matter underscored, old matter in brackets, and words divided only where real prints divide them. It runs here on this computer; the first time, the typesetter takes a moment to load.

        The Bank on this computer

        Pick the law files the NYS Law Library downloads (one .json per law, e.g. EDN - Education.json). They are kept in Word's storage on this computer only; nothing is sent anywhere.
